Speed Riddle #84 +25 XP

If inflation is 1.5% and your savings account yields 0.75%, are you gaining or losing purchasing power?

Correct! Because prices rise by 1.5% while your money only grows by 0.75%, each dollar buys slightly less next year. That's why investing beats idle cash.
