Investing Sandbox

Practise investing with pretend money — zero real risk. Pick an asset, choose an amount, then Buy or Sell. Use Advance simulation to see how prices move over time and watch your portfolio react.

lock No real money
help How does this work?expand_more
  1. You start with pretend cash. Your portfolio value = cash + what your holdings are worth right now.
  2. Pick an asset. Each has a different risk level — steadier ones (like a savings bond) barely move; exciting ones swing more.
  3. Buy or sell. Your money converts into shares at the current price. Selling turns shares back into cash.
  4. Advance the simulation. Each step nudges every price up or down a little (bigger swings for riskier assets, always capped so you can't be wiped out). The chart shows that real price history.
  5. Watch profit & loss. It's the difference between what you paid on average and the current price — the heart of learning to invest.

It's a learning tool: no real money, no real companies, and prices are a simplified simulation — not real markets.
